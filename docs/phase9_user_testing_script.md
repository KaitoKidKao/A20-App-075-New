# Phase 9 - Kich ban test voi nguoi dung muc tieu

## Doi tuong moi

- 3-5 hoc sinh/sinh vien khiem thinh hoac nang thinh.
- 1-2 giao vien ho tro giao duc dac biet.
- Neu co the, moi them 1 phien dich vien ngon ngu ky hieu de review VSL gloss/avatar.

## Nguyen tac test

- Khong giai thich truoc cach dung qua nhieu. Hay de nguoi dung tu thao tac.
- Quan sat hanh vi, khong chi nghe nhan xet.
- Uu tien ghi lai diem gay kho: caption, dieu huong, toc do doc, layout mobile, glossary/avatar.
- Khong coi avatar AI la ket qua ngon ngu ky hieu chinh thuc neu chua co chuyen gia review.

## Kich ban 45 phut

### 1. Mo dau - 5 phut

Hoi nhanh:

- Ban thuong hoc bang video tren thiet bi nao?
- Ban uu tien phu de, transcript hay ngon ngu ky hieu?
- Ban co hay dung ban phim/screen reader/phong to chu khong?

### 2. Nhiem vu hoc bang video - 15 phut

Yeu cau nguoi dung:

1. Dang nhap.
2. Mo mot bai hoc video.
3. Bat/tat caption.
4. Doi caption `VI` va `EN`.
5. Doi co chu/vi tri caption.
6. Click transcript de tua video.
7. Tam dung va quay lai hoc tiep.

Can quan sat:

- Co tim thay nut doi ngon ngu caption khong?
- Caption co doc kip khong?
- Transcript co giup hoc nhanh hon khong?
- Vi tri resume co dung ky vong khong?

### 3. Nhiem vu on tap - 10 phut

Yeu cau:

1. Mo tab flashcard.
2. Lat the.
3. Bam `Da nho` hoac `Can on lai`.
4. Mo dashboard student.
5. Tim bai dang hoc do.

Can quan sat:

- Nguoi dung co hieu y nghia 2 nut on tap khong?
- Dashboard co cho biet tiep tuc hoc o dau khong?
- Thong tin co qua day/qua it khong?

### 4. VSL/avatar - 10 phut

Yeu cau:

1. Mo tab Avatar VSL.
2. Xem chuoi gloss.
3. Tai manifest.
4. Neu co token, generate avatar.

Hoi:

- Disclaimer co ro rang khong?
- Avatar/gloss co huu ich khong?
- Neu ky hieu sai, ban muon bao loi/sua o dau?

### 5. Tong ket - 5 phut

Hoi diem so 1-5:

- De dung tong the.
- De doc caption.
- De theo doi transcript.
- Tin cay cua noi dung AI.
- Muc do huu ich cua flashcard/quiz.
- Muc do huu ich cua VSL/avatar.

## Tieu chi Go/No-Go

Co the release beta neu:

- Khong co bug blocker trong login, xem video, caption song ngu.
- Caption doc duoc tren mobile va desktop.
- Student co the quay lai hoc tiep dung vi tri.
- Teacher/admin xem duoc dashboard loi/tien do co ban.
- Nguoi dung muc tieu khong gap rao can nghiem trong khi dung keyboard/caption.

Khong nen release neu:

- Caption sai timeline hoac tron ngon ngu.
- User xem duoc du lieu cua user khac.
- Upload lam server crash.
- UI mobile che caption hoac khong dieu khien duoc video.
